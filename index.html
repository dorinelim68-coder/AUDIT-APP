How issues are scored

Score = risk level (High 60, Medium 40, Low 20) + up to 30 for the amount relative to performance materiality + 5 for fraud and going-concern categories.

Priority: P1 at 75 or more, P2 at 50 to 74, P3 below 50. You can override priority on any issue.
